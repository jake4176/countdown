FAQ

Why is Pomodoro 25 minutes?

25 minutes is a common starting length — short enough to begin easily, long enough for a real work block. Treat it as a default and adjust to fit you.

Does a study timer improve focus?

A timer clarifies when you start and stop. It does not magically increase focus, but it makes starting easier and time visible, which helps habits.

Can I pause the timer?

Yes. Pause or reset when life interrupts. Getting back into a session matters more than unbroken perfection.

What should I do on a break?

Step away from the screen, move a little, or drink water. High-stimulation breaks can make the next focus block harder.

Are my focus logs stored on a server?

No. Everything stays in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes your history.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is responsive. If a mobile browser throttles background tabs, remaining time is recalculated from the real end time.
